# Jarvis 0.1.3: setup on your Redmi 9

Install the new APK as an update over Jarvis. Keep the existing app installed to preserve its saved key, memories and downloaded packs. The package and certificate are unchanged. This is still a development preview, not a Google-approved release.

## 1. Verify Gemini, rather than just saving a key

Open **Settings → Gemini → Find Gemini models**. Choose a listed **Flash-Lite** or **Flash** text model for a faster starting point. Exact availability and quota depend on your Google project.

Tap **Test connection**. A visible dialog shows elapsed time and **Cancel check**. It makes a short, real API request with a 15-second deadline. Success shows **Connection verified**, an actual sample answer and elapsed time. Tap **Connect & save**. Failure shows **Connection not verified**, with a fixed explanation/status code. No saved-key indicator or model list is treated as a successful test.

Use your Google AI Studio key privately in the app. Do not send it in chat. The test sends no conversation, saved facts, files, notifications or screen data.

## 2. Start speech sooner

Enable **Speak replies**. Keep **OpenAI cloud voice** off to use a system or downloaded on-device voice and avoid an additional cloud speech request. Run the language's voice check; download its offline voice if the phone lacks one.

General AI text streams into short speech chunks instead of waiting for the full answer. Known Gemini 2.5 Flash models use a zero thinking budget; that parameter is not forced onto unknown model families. Models, provider queues, network conditions, voice initialization and Redmi CPU speed still determine the real delay. Immediate answers cannot be guaranteed. The local 0.5B brain can be much slower than Gemini on this phone.

## 3. Capture a screen and ask about it

Enable **Screen reading · Accessibility** in Jarvis Settings and Android's access screen. Start the selected language's wake listening, then open the page you want analyzed.

Say **“Hey Jarvis, analyze my screen”** or **“Hey Jarvis, scan my screen.”** Spanish examples: **“Hola Jarvis, analiza mi pantalla.”** Persian: **“سلام جارویس، صفحه را تحلیل کن.”** Only the selected recognition language is active.

Accept Jarvis's capture disclosure and Android's one-shot screen-capture prompt. On Android 10, this system consent cannot be skipped. Jarvis captures one image, stops projection, and asks what you want to know. With wake listening active, a 12-second follow-up window opens after that prompt; you can say **“summarize this”**, **“explain this”**, or **“give me a little information about this.”** Later, use the wake phrase again and refer to the screen.

The app shows **Screen snapshot ready**, plus **View** and **Clear screen**. One JPEG stays in memory for up to two minutes. Clearing, screen-off/lock, Stop everything, replacement or an unrelated question removes it. A cloud screen follow-up sends that image to Gemini only after the capture disclosure is accepted. Other cloud providers receive visible text only; the local model also uses only visible text. Protected/password screens are rejected, and secure-window protections are not bypassed. Image analysis may make mistakes; check important details.

## 4. Use memory deliberately

Say **“Remember that my favorite color is teal.”** Explicit facts survive app restarts and can be viewed/deleted in Memory. To let Gemini use those facts in answers, enable **Use saved memories with cloud AI**, then **Save settings**. It is off by default and shares at most 20 facts / 5,000 characters with questions.

Recent general chat retains up to four turns in RAM for follow-up questions. It does not include phone tools, screenshot answers or file answers. It is cleared when the screen turns off, on relevant privacy/provider changes, or when conversation/data is cleared. Durable facts and temporary conversation context are different.

## 5. Screen-off and locked-phone use

Download the selected speech pack, start listening while Jarvis is open and the phone is unlocked, then enable **General chat while locked → Save settings** if you want public answers while locked. Nearby people can also ask general questions and hear those answers. Private information, screenshots and phone actions still require unlock; locked general requests omit your saved name, facts, history and attachments.

Active listening has a visible microphone notification and keeps the CPU awake, using battery. MIUI may need **Phone Settings → Apps → Jarvis → Battery saver → No restrictions**; labels vary. Android may recover an already-active session after process death, but it can still stop listening. After reboot or Force stop, reopen Jarvis and tap Start listening.

**A powered-off phone cannot run Jarvis.** This release does not claim perpetual background operation, boot-time microphone access, voice interruption during replies, or a floating overlay.

Your account-backed connection, physical microphone, wake accuracy, audible voice quality, battery endurance and measured Redmi latency must still be verified on the phone.
