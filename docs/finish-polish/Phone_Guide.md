# Jarvis combined polish update — phone guide

Install over the matching newer Jarvis app. Package/signing identity stay unchanged; preserve installed data if Android rejects a certificate. Do not uninstall to force an update.

Open **Settings → Rebirth → Intelligence & Systems**. This lives inside the existing app; no new core module or home-screen replacement is involved.

1. **Voice/local answers:** enable normal wake listening. Try “Jarvis, are you up?”, then “What's your name?”, “What time zone is my phone using?” and “What is two plus three times four?” Also try “Hey Jarvis, what time is it?” in one breath. Optional custom wake phrases are under Voice health; they must not collide with system/learned phrases. Free-form offline questions still need an installed local brain.
2. **System Doctor:** tap Scan/Check, read measured versus unknown evidence. A proposed balanced-settings repair requires review/approval. A settings-readback success is followed by your real voice test. Roll back if desired. OS permission buttons open Android's settings; nothing silently grants access.
3. **Teach Jarvis:** enable explicit personal learning, enter a title and aliases (one per line), choose actual installed Android apps or supported Chrome/search steps on the current paired computer. Review the full plan, approve Save, then Try. You may say “When I say coding time, open Chrome on my computer”; this opens a review, not an automatic saved automation. Edit, undo, disable, delete and explicit private export/import remain available. Vocabulary notes are notes, not acoustic model training.
4. **Future Actions:** opt in to battery, explicit routines and/or Calendar separately. Calendar also needs Android READ_CALENDAR permission. Configure zone, lead time, quiet hours, daily cap, delivery and any approved recipe. One actual suggestion appears while the app is visible and idle. Review it before execution, or use Not now, Snooze, Never and full opt-out. Process/background/exact timing is not guaranteed; use existing dated Call-me check-ins for supported scheduled alerts.
5. **Mission Control:** open the companion on the trusted computer and pair with the existing QR/code flow if needed. Check actual capabilities, set a friendly name, review Chrome/search, confirm and inspect the real progress/result. A computer with no speakers/mic still receives approved commands while the phone handles speech. Unsupported app/file actions are unavailable, not pretend buttons. Cancel cannot undo a page already opened. [Computer setup](../../companion/README.md).
6. **Core:** original geometry/layout stays; actual speaking is blue, listening purple, idle cyan, and green charging accents require true charging. Full-but-not-charging has its full status/notice without pretending to charge. Respect system reduced-motion and wallpaper visibility. [Existing moving-wallpaper setup](../v2/PHONE-GUIDE.md).

Physical acceptance: repeat 100 acoustic wake attempts, 30 follow-ups and20 combined phrases on the actual phone; record successes, false positives and actual timing. Test lock/Home/another app, denied mic, busy audio, missing model/backend, Stop/force-stop, actual plug/unplug/full state, optional owner filter, each approved recipe/cancel/change, opt-out and desktop disconnection. These tests were not replaced with logical unit loops.

No Video6158 was attached, so the supplied descriptions are test targets rather than a reproduced video timeline. Full test/build counts and remaining scope are in the release verification and Feature_Matrix.md.
