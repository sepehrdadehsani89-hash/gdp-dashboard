Jarvis Sentinel procedural sound effects

The 26 sentinel_*.wav assets are original deterministic FM/sine synthesis generated
by scripts/generate-sentinel-sounds.py, with no recordings, third-party samples,
or movie effects. They are made available under CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Playback is governed by the user's effects volume, silent/Do Not Disturb settings,
microphone capture, speech and audio-focus policy. Physical sound quality still
requires listening on the target phone.
